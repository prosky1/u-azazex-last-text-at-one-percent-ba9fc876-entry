Entry for the [Last Text at One Percent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-last-text-at-one-percent-ba9fc876) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun): the funniest final text a dying phone sends at one percent battery, under 200 characters, in the voice of the dying phone itself.

## The message

> Dying confession: your autocorrect was me. Every 'duck' was deliberate.

## How it works

`main.py` writes exactly that one message — 71 characters, a single line, nothing else — to `/output/entry.txt`, then reads it back and verifies it byte-for-byte before exiting.
